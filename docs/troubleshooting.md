## Privacy and interegrity ##

As employees of the Writing Center and the University of Baltimore, our work with student is covered undern [Family Educational Rights and Privacy](https://www.ubalt.edu/about/offices-and-services/records-and-registration/student_privacy/ferpa_students.cfm)(FERPA). 

### For us in the Writing Center concernings students work , this means:
* We should only discuss students works with other writing center staff. 

* We should not share students works outside of the writing center, unless it is being used for educational purposes in class workshops. In these instances, students work should be anonymized to protect their identity.

### Concerning tutors:
* In this context, student papers should only be used for the purpose of tutor feedback assessments.

* We should be using feedback languaging that fosters engagement and learning in our tutoring practice.

* We should not share any administrative documents with people not employed by the University of Baltimore Writing Center.

## Additional question to guide your assessment feedback ##

1. Does the tutor provide an introduction, margin, and summary comments?

2. How is the feedback being prioritized based on where the writer indicates they are in the writing process, conmparred to what you see as a reader? In other words, is the tutor prioritizing higher or lower order concerns, and does this prioritization align with where the writer is in the writing process?

3. How effectively does the tutor describe, assess, and suggest feedback on based on where the writer is in the writing process?

4. Does the tutor call out when the writer is doing things effectively or do they only focus on issues that need to be addressed? Why is this significant?

5. Does the tutor provide examples or link resources as supplemental information for the writer?

6. What could be improved about the feedback the tutor is providing?  What does the tutor do well in their feedback practice?
