# Asychronous Observation Assessement Guide #

This document will help writig tutors follows the steps to complete the asynchronus oberservation assessment process. 

## Problem ##

Tutoring at our writing center has largely taken place online through the Appointment Tool and Online Writing Lab (OWL) since 2020. One of the requirements of College Reading and Learning Association (CRLA) certified tutoring center is that tutors are observed in practice as part of their training and development. 

Over these six years, this assessment has taken place each semester as part of our training weeks. The prompts that guide you throught this process are scattered throughout out Writing Center TEAMS notebook. In order to do these oberservations, you'll need to navigate across multiple applications in a series of steps to complete the full assessment process. In an effort to formalize this documentation process, and more actively facilitate peer mentorship and transmission of knowledges, I created this Asychrnous Oberservation Assessment Guide.

## Audience ##

This guide is for writing tutors who are:
 1. CRLA level two and level three certified
 2. New tutors in the writng center as part particpating in training week(s) about obersvations

This guide is also for writing center administrator interested in training models for peer-led asynchronus observation assessement.

# Purpose #

Asynchronus observations are one way to assess the feedback we provide to students about their writing. I want to help you to learn new skills, apply your knowledges and contribute to the commnutity of practice in our writing center (Green, 2007, p. 50). Part of fostering culture of learning means moving away from from a hierarchy that locks you into the role of the tutor, only qualified to work with students. There is so much more work to do in our writing center community. In order to expand the boundaries of what it means to be a tutor, your identity as a tutor has to expand too. Learning the asychronous observations assessment process is one of the way to expand your identity as a tutor. This guide will help tutors:

* Locate and use the apps needed to complete the asychronous oberservation assessment process.

* Follow step-by-step instructions for retrieving and assessing tutors' asychronous feedback practices.

* Learn file naming conventions and storage requirements.

* Troubleshoot FAQs

* Understand matters of privacy, authorized acceess and the responsible handling of downloading files
