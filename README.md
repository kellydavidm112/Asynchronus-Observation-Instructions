# Asychronous Observation Assessement Guide #

This guide will help writing tutors follows the steps to complete the asynchronus oberservation assessment process. 

## Problem ##

Tutoring at our writing center has primarily taken place online through the Appointment Tool and Online Writing Lab (OWL) since 2020. Our writing center does not have written processes for online (sychronous and asynchronous) obersevations, thus creating inconsistencies in, you the tutors, being observed.One of the requirements of [College Reading and Learning Association](https://crla.net/page/ITTPC) (CRLA) certified tutoring center is that tutors are observed in practice as part of their training and development. 

This guide started as an idea, a topic for one of the writing center staff trainings. Over a number of semester I drafted and revised the asynchronous observations training (also know nas our OWL feedback training)
The prompts that guide you through this process are scattered throughout our Writing Center TEAMS notebook, since I dont do each training exactly the same. 

In order to do these oberservations assessment, you need to navigate across multiple applications in a series of steps to complete the full assessment process. In an effort to formalize this documentation process, and more actively facilitate peer mentorship and transmission of knowledges, I created this Asychronous Oberservation Assessment Guide.

## Audience ##

This guide is for:

 1. CRLA level two and level three certified writing tutors.

 2. New tutors in the writng center particpating in training week(s) about obersvations.

 3. Writing Center Administrators interested in training models for peer-led asynchronus observation assessement.

## Purpose ##

Asynchronus observations are one way to assess the feedback we provide to students about their writing. I want to help you learn new skills, apply your knowledges and contribute to the commnutity of practice in our writing center (Green, 2007, p. 50). Part of fostering culture of learning means moving away from from a hierarchy that locks you into the role of the tutor, only qualified to work with students in writing consultations. There is so much more work to do in our writing center community. 

In order to expand the boundaries of what it means to be a tutor, your identity as a tutor has to expand too. Learning the asychronous observations assessment process is one of the way to expand your identity as a tutor. This guide will help you:

* Locate and use the apps needed to complete the asychronous oberservation assessment process.

* Follow step-by-step instructions for retrieving and assessing tutors' asychronous feedback practices.

* Learn file naming conventions and storage requirements.

* Troubleshoot other considerations (eg. privacy, feedback guidance, authorized access).
